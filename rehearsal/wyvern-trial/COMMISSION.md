# COMMISSION — wyvern authoring trial (trial 3)

Author a new creature on the golem session surface: a TRUE WYVERN. Bipedal —
the forelimbs ARE the wings, no separate arms; two hind legs bear the stance;
a long counterweight tail. This bauplan is deliberately distinct from the
verdigris hexapod drake: it forces balance over two feet, wing-as-forelimb
terminal circulation, and membrane spanning 3+ wing fingers.

This is the FIRST trial against the wave-3 amended surface. The friction
journal is as much the deliverable as the creature: it measures whether
wave 3 (roles, webs, carves, material classes, verdict truthfulness, one
acceptance oracle) actually cured what the first two trials paid for.

## Scope & conventions

- Spec at `specs/<name>.json` — christen the creature yourself. All trial
  artifacts live in `rehearsal/wyvern-trial/` (this directory): `FRICTION.md`
  (contemporaneous, exact command + verdict fragment at the moment of
  confusion), `verdict-NN.json` append-only, `base.json`, look renders —
  per the two prior trials' conventions.
- Carry the scars: read `rehearsal/scree-maiden-trial/FRICTION.md` and
  `rehearsal/verdigris-trial/FRICTION.md` FIRST. A wall those journals
  already name is a scar — apply the recorded cure silently; only NEW walls
  earn entries. Build in stages (verdigris 000): skeleton + carrier flesh
  and vascular acceptance first, decorative flesh next, attach pass, tint
  last. Frame-probe every multi-bone chain before fleshing it; remember the
  axial rest_dir law (verdigris 001) — the tail is where it bites.
- Exercise the wave-3 vocabulary wherever the form honestly wants it:
  `webs` spanning the wing fingers (the (A-1)^2 arity case — use 3+ anchors),
  `role: non_carrier` flesh, `handle` bones for decorative spikes/orientation,
  `carves` (mouth or nostrils), material classes if the look calls for them.
- KNOWN-OPEN WALL, not a fresh finding: vascular consumption of webs is
  deferred (verdigris A13d). Wing venation cannot yet be lawful vasculature —
  route wing-tip exchange through carrier flesh struts; membranes ride as
  non_carrier webs.
- Environment: invoke the kernel via `.venv/bin/python -m golem ...` directly —
  `uv run` panics under the sandbox (verdigris 012). Set
  `GOLEM_CIRCUIT_CACHE_COLD=1` while iterating vascular seeds (verdigris 002).
- Acceptance: the `session` verdict is the canonical oracle — accepted status
  AND a read of the headline diagnostics, never exit codes (verdigris 011);
  `check` should agree (wave-3 B1 — if it disagrees, that is a journal entry).
  The bipedal stance must clear `env_balanced` (centroid over the two-foot
  support polygon) honestly — no undeclared wing-knuckle props.
- Do NOT modify the kernel, session, senses, or contract code. Trials are
  authoring-only; if the surface is wrong, the journal entry is the
  deliverable — author around the wall honestly and record it.
