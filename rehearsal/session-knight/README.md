## What happened

The operator authored a 14-bone, 19-flesh symmetric knight in ~30 tool calls:
upright column, mirrored arms and legs via `reflect` (never authored a right
side), envelope contracts (heads-tall, balance, single-component) all passing
at the end, feet planted by the ground solve. Most defects — sword-through-
torso, legs crossing the midline, balance — were diagnosed and fixed FROM
RECEIPT NUMBERS without spending renders; the six renders were mostly
confirmations. That inversion (text loop primary, perception occasional) is
the session thesis working as designed.

## Tool findings (full detail in OPERATOR_NOTES.md — worth reading whole)

Receipts and `reflect` and `world_dir` and `envelope` carried the build.
Four reaches into thin air, all lesson-register candidates for the spec:
world-space/bbox introspection (an `outline ... geometry` aspect), per-bone
frame-axis introspection (parent-frame `offset` is unauthorable blind — the
operator invented fleshless `world_dir` stub bones as a workaround, which is
the dialect telling us what it wants), an explicit weld/join (single-component
integrity is emergent from flesh girth, so a realistic thin blade split the
creature — the substrate cannot yet say "these two parts are joined"), and a
legible heads-tall operand (shrinking the helm moved nothing; the ruler must
be inspectable). The mount/port surface was undocumented in HELP, so hands are
mitts — same as every prior probe.
